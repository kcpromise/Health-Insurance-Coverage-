# Health-Insurance-Coverage

Health Insurance Coverage Dashboard

📊 Project Overview
The Health Insurance Coverage Dashboard is a Power BI data analytics project designed to analyze health insurance coverage, Medicaid enrollment, Medicare enrollment, uninsured rates and tax credit information across U.S. states.
The dashboard provides interactive insights into insurance enrollment, uninsured populations, healthcare coverage changes and tax credit distribution across different states and years.

🎯 Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze health insurance coverage data and identify patterns and changes in insurance enrollment, uninsured rates and healthcare coverage across U.S. states.
The analysis is designed to help stakeholders understand healthcare coverage trends, Medicaid and Medicare enrollment, and changes in uninsured rates over time.

🔍 Key Insights
The analysis provides insights into differences in health insurance coverage across U.S. states.
The dashboard highlights changes in uninsured rates over time, Medicaid and Medicare enrollment levels, health insurance coverage growth and the distribution of marketplace tax credits.
It also allows users to compare state-level healthcare coverage and identify states with relatively higher or lower insurance enrollment and uninsured rates.
